# Reach

This site exists so the project's own name, and the questions officials and
IT staff type, have a page to land on. The objective, the questions and the
weekly measurements live in the software repository:
[OSGovt/open-county-portal/REACH.md](https://github.com/OSGovt/open-county-portal/blob/main/REACH.md)
and its `reach/` folder, where `osgovt.com` is one of the measured
properties (Search Console Domain property `sc-domain:osgovt.com`).

The weekly maintenance pass may change pages here to answer one of those
questions better. The rule is the same as everywhere in the family: every
claim traces to a file in `open-county-portal`; no compliance promises, no
dates or counts not found there; no tracking scripts.
